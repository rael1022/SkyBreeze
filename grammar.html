🌬️ Grammar Breeze · Lesson 4

Let the sentence flow

Follow the breeze from a place noun to an everyday Korean expression. Tap each card’s tip to make the pattern stick.

BREEZE 01

N에 가요 / 와요

Go to / come to a place. Use 에 after the destination.

Destination
A

어디에 가요?

B

우체국에 가요.

Think of 에 as an arrow pointing to your destination: 학교에 가요 — “I go to school.”

BREEZE 02

N에 있어요 / 없어요

Say that something exists, or does not exist, in a place.

Existence
A

서울타워가 어디에 있어요?

B

남산에 있어요.

있어요 means “there is / exists.” Change it to 없어요 to say “there is not / does not exist.”
