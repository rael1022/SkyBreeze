Board a Korean speaking journey

Each role-play has three speakers and six lines. Read the Korean, use the English translation when needed, then record your group’s 1–3 minute natural short drama.

🛫

Role Play 01 · Airport Journey

Meeting a friend at the airport

A: 어디에 가요?Where are you going?

B: 공항에 가요. 친구를 마중하러 가요.I am going to the airport to meet my friend.

C: 저도 공항에 가요. 가족이 한국에 와요.I am going to the airport too. My family is coming to Korea.

A: 몇 시에 가요?What time are you going?

B: 오후 세 시에 가요.I am going at three in the afternoon.

C: 좋아요. 같이 가요!Great. Let’s go together!

Members 1 + 2 + 3
6 lines · 1–3 min
🗺️

Role Play 02 · Finding Places

Looking for the hospital

A: 병원이 어디에 있어요?Where is the hospital?

B: 은행 옆에 있어요.It is beside the bank.

C: 약국 앞에 있어요?Is it in front of the pharmacy?

B: 아니요, 약국 뒤에 있어요.No, it is behind the pharmacy.

A: 아, 알겠어요. 감사합니다.Ah, I understand. Thank you.

C: 제가 같이 갈게요.I will go with you.

Members 4 + 5 + 6
6 lines · 1–3 min
🏫

Role Play 03 · Campus Location

Study, then visit a cafe

A: 도서관이 어디에 있어요?Where is the library?

B: 학생식당 앞에 있어요.It is in front of the student cafeteria.

C: 도서관에서 한국어를 공부해요?Do you study Korean at the library?

A: 네, 친구와 같이 공부해요.Yes, I study with a friend.

B: 저는 도서관 옆 카페에 가요.I am going to the cafe beside the library.

C: 좋아요. 수업 후에 같이 가요!Great. Let’s go together after class!

Members 1 + 3 + 5
6 lines · 1–3 min

🎬 Record each role play as a 1–3 minute short drama. Every recording should show all three members’ faces and voices, natural acting, Korean speaking and English subtitles.
